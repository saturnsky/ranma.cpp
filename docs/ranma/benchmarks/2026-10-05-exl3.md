# Qwen3.8-Flash-Next in EXL3 against UD-Q4_K_XL

- Release: `ranma_20261005`
- Upstream base: `42d958167`
- Measured: 2026-10-05
- Method: [method.md](method.md); the settings of these rows below.

Qwen3.8-Flash-Next in three quantizations: EXL3 3.05 bpw (`Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-EXL3-3.05bpw-h5-ng5`), EXL3 4.05 bpw
(`Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-EXL3-4.05bpw-h6-ng6`) and UD-Q4_K_XL, on the Radeon AI PRO R9700 (PCIe 5.0 x16, -30 % power
limit, 0 mV voltage offset, headless, ROCm 10 runtime next to the binaries). The EXL3 files are those of
[the Hugging Face repository](https://huggingface.co/SaturnHeaven/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-RANMA-EXL3-GGUF); the split files
and a single file hold the same tensors. Three systems, emulated with the expert cache alone (no `-ncmoe`,
`--expert-cache-mode exclusive`): R9700 with 128 GB (`--expert-l1-mib 20480 --expert-l2-mib -1`), R9700 with 64 GB
(`20480`, `40960`) and the RX 9070 XT emulation with 64 GB (`3072`, `40960`; the same R9700 with the budget of a
16 GiB card, as in [the full set](2026-09-22-full-set.md#protocol-of-the-full-set); the device buffers outside the cache
stay under 15 GiB, 11.3 to 12.4 GiB measured). Every process starts after a 300 s rest, one GPU process at a time, each
row measured once.

**`llama-bench`** rows: the command of [method.md](method.md#llama-bench-rows) (`-d 65536,0,4096,8192,32768,65536`, first
pair discarded). Cold runs the whole process from the seeded placement and writes the profile; every warm row starts from
its own copy of the cold profile of its model and runs with `--expert-prefill-swap`.

**Server** rows: the server row and the presets of [method.md](method.md#server-rows) (context 65536, greedy, at most 384
tokens), MTP with the draft `mtp-Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-Q8_0.gguf` (ggml-org), `-ngld 999 --spec-type draft-mtp --spec-smart
--expert-cache-draft on`, prefill swap off, `GGML_CUDA_TOP_K_STABLE_TIES=1` and `ROCBLAS_USE_HIPBLASLT=0` as in
[the MTP record](2026-10-01-mtp-smart.md). Each scenario and system first runs its warm-up preset in one process from an
empty profile and an empty `--spec-smart-store` (not in the tables, no rest before it); the server exits normally and saves
the store. The evaluation row then starts from copies of that profile and that store. There are no cold server rows.

## Reading the tables

- EXL3 3.05 bpw is faster than UD-Q4_K_XL on every system, and the difference grows as fewer experts stay in VRAM and host
  memory: `llama-bench` TG128 at depth 0 and 65536 +5.9 to +9.5 % on the R9700, +17.7 to +20.5 % on the RX 9070 XT
  emulation; server decode with MTP +17.2 to +23.1 % on the R9700 with 128 GB, +15.2 to +45.2 % with 64 GB and +50.6
  to +85.9 % on the RX 9070 XT emulation.
- EXL3 4.05 bpw decodes like UD-Q4_K_XL in `llama-bench` on the R9700 (TG128 at depth 0 and 65536 within 1 %) and
  processes prompts faster (PP512 +1.6 to +9.5 %); server decode with MTP is +3.9 to +10.0 % on the R9700 with 128 GB and +15.4 to +28.1 % on the
  RX 9070 XT emulation.
- With 64 GB the host-side experts of EXL3 3.05 bpw (about 23 GiB outside the 20 GiB VRAM budget) fit in the host tier
  of 40 GiB, so its placement is that of 128 GB; the PP512 difference at depth 0 (1433 against 1255) is between two
  processes, each measured once. The host-side experts of UD-Q4_K_XL (about 52 GiB) do not fit in that tier, which
  is why its 64 GB coding row (45.71 t/s) is below the 128 GB one (53.89).
- The acceptance of the drafted tokens is not a ranking: `--spec-smart` chooses the draft length per system and model.
- On the RX 9070 XT emulation TG128 at depth 0 is lower than at depth 4096, as in [the full set](2026-09-22-full-set.md);
  this was not diagnosed.
- The server numbers are not comparable with [the MTP record](2026-10-01-mtp-smart.md): the draft head (the ggml-org
  Q8_0 here, a shared Q8_0 there), the series and the way the smart store is warmed differ.
